Explore the approach ↘

The cost of delay is real.
So is the opportunity to act.

When a critical deliverable falls behind, external costs rise and internal teams face competing demands. We are developing an operator-led approach to connect the budget for a priority with the people and work required to deliver it.

The approach

From priority to shared outcome.

01 / DEFINE

Set the outcome

Clarify the deliverable, the economics at stake and the criteria for success.

02 / ALIGN

Connect budget and work

Structure the work, capacity and proposed incentives around a common objective.

03 / EXECUTE

Coordinate delivery

Bring sponsors, operators and teams together to agree on a practical path forward.

An illustrative mandate

An overdue audit.
A different allocation.

A portfolio company faces an overdue audit package and further external support costs. A defined delivery mandate could connect an incentive pool to the internal work needed to bring the package to review readiness.

The proposed outcome

A review-ready evidence package.

Reconcile outstanding balances, assemble supporting evidence and prepare a handover for the auditor. The sponsor, operator and finance team agree on scope, capacity, incentives and acceptance criteria.

The partnership

A shared mandate.
Clear ownership.

The sponsor defines the priority and available budget. The operator structures the outcome and coordinates delivery. The team brings the expertise and discusses the proposed work and incentives.

The planned software would make the economics, work packages and proposed terms visible in one place. An AI agent could help structure information and surface dependencies; human participants and authorized approvers remain responsible for decisions. This is a concept in development.

Start with one critical outcome.

Audit readiness, overdue reporting or another high-priority finance deliverable. Define the mandate, test the economics and build the approach around the people doing the work.
